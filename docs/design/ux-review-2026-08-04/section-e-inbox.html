Answering in place

L'idea della inbox è di dar modo all'utente di rispondere al volo a tutte le sessioni che hanno bisogno di lui senza dover ogni volta andare alla sessione in questione.

Confirmed: onResolve and onReply already answer without navigating (AttentionInbox.svelte:187-193, :194-218). What happens right after isn't designed, it's a side effect of how the list is built: every session subscribes both its transcript and its permission queue back into recomputeAttentionInbox (relay-client.ts:3461-3462), which rebuilds the whole array from scratch (:3472-3514). The instant resolvePermission removes the head of a queue (:2503), or a reply moves a session off awaiting_input, that row is gone on the very next tick — no confirmation, no undo, nothing shown for what just happened. A session that only finished has no answer to give and, today, no dismiss action either: it sits in the inbox until you open it.
